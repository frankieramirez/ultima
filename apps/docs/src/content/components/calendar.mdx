# Calendar

An inline day, month, and year grid on Zag's date-picker machine: `Root` runs the machine with `inline` pinned, `Content` wraps the views, and `Table` draws the grid for whichever `view` it is given. Zag owns selection, focus, and keyboard; Ultima paints the panel, the navigation, and the cells. For the input-and-popup composition, use Date Picker.

## Install

```bash
npx shadcn add @ultima/calendar
```

## The grid is the control

`Calendar.Label` names the grid; render one, or give `Table` an `aria-label` of your own. `ViewControl` holds the navigation: `PrevTrigger` and `NextTrigger` step the visible month, `ViewTrigger` cycles the view between day, month, and year, and `RangeText` inside it shows the visible range. `Table` renders its own `TableHead` and `TableBody` when you give it no children, including the weekday headers and one `TableCellTrigger` button per day.

<Demo component={Basic} source={basicSource} />

## Choosing a range

`selectionMode="range"` turns the grid into a range picker: the first click marks `data-range-start`, the second `data-range-end`, and every day between them carries `data-in-range`. The cell paint reads all three attributes, plus `data-selected` for single days, `data-today`, `data-outside-range`, and `data-unavailable`.

<Demo component={Range} source={rangeSource} />

## Month and year views

`Table` takes `view="month"` or `view="year"` and enumerates the same way: twelve month cells or a decade of year cells. `ViewTrigger` cycles the machine through all three views on click. If the visible month or year should be chosen directly, `MonthSelect` and `YearSelect` are the native selects the machine already wires; they keep their platform look.

<Demo component={Selects} source={selectsSource} />

## More than one month

`numOfMonths` on `Root` widens the visible range. Each month is its own `Table`; `TableBody` takes `monthsOffset` so the second table enumerates the following month's weeks.

## Props

Everything on `Root` is a Zag date-picker machine prop; `inline` is pinned, and the popup and form props (`open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `positioning`, `closeOnSelect`, `name`, `required`, `placeholder`) are gone from the type. See [Zag's date-picker](https://zagjs.com/components/react/date-picker) for the inherited surface.

| Part | Prop | Type | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Root` | `selectionMode` | `'single' \| 'multiple' \| 'range'` | `'single'` | What a click commits. |
| `Root` | `value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange` | `DateValue[]` | `[]` | `@internationalized/date` values. |
| `Root` | `numOfMonths` | `number` | `1` | How many months the visible range spans. |
| `Root` | `min`, `max` | `DateValue` | — | Bounds the selectable range. |
| `Root` | `isDateUnavailable` | `(date, locale) => boolean` | — | Blocks a date from selection. |
| `Root` | `disabled`, `readOnly` | `boolean` | `false` | Machine-wide state. |
| `Root` | `locale`, `timeZone`, `startOfWeek`, `fixedWeeks`, `showWeekNumbers` | — | — | Grid shape and locale. |
| `ViewControl`, `PrevTrigger`, `NextTrigger`, `ViewTrigger`, `View`, `Table`, `TableHead`, `TableBody`, `TableRow`, `TableHeader`, `TableCell`, `TableCellTrigger` | `view` | `'day' \| 'month' \| 'year'` | `'day'` | Which view the part's getter answers for. |
| `Table` | `columns` | `number` | `7` or `4` | Grid columns; month and year views default to four. |
| `TableBody` | `monthsOffset` | `number` | `0` | Which month of `numOfMonths` the body enumerates. |
| `TableCell`, `TableCellTrigger` | `value` | `DateValue \| number` | — | The day, month, or year the cell stands for. |
| Every part | `style` | `StyleXStyles` | — | The style slot, merged last. There is no `className` and no `render`. |

## Accessibility

- **Name.** `Label` names the grid: `Table` points `aria-labelledby` at it unless you give the table its own `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- **Landmarks.** `Table` is `role="grid"` with `<th scope="col">` column headers, `role="gridcell"` cells, and `role="rowheader"` week-number cells.
- **Focus ring.** On `PrevTrigger`, `NextTrigger`, `ViewTrigger`, `TableCellTrigger`, `MonthSelect`, and `YearSelect`, the styled controls.
- **Keyboard.** Zag's grid map: arrows move the focused day, Page Up and Page Down change the month, Home and End the week, Enter and Space select.
- **Disabled.** A disabled day keeps `aria-disabled` and `data-disabled`; it is skipped, not focusable.
